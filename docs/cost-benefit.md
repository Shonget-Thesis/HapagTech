## ROI Calculation

*Formula: ROI = (Benefits - Costs) / Costs × 100*

**Total Year 1 Investment Cost:** ₱594,400 (Dev ₱269,200 + Ops ₱325,200)

### Scenario A: Conservative (8 Restaurants)

| Metric | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Annual Revenue | ₱240,000 |
| Annual Costs | ₱594,400 |
| Benefits (Revenue) | ₱240,000 |
| ROI | (240,000 - 594,400) / 594,400 × 100 = **-59.6%** |
| Break-even | Not achieved Year 1 |
| Assessment | Not viable with 8 restaurants |

## Recommendation

**Status: PROCEED (with conditions)**

**Rationale:**

*Positive factors:*
- Break-even achievable by end of Year 1 (11.5 months) in realistic scenario
- Positive ROI by Year 2-3 with consistent 40% annual growth
- Low upfront development cost (₱269k) amortized across operations
- Philippine team reduces costs 40-60% vs. offshore alternatives
- Subscription model provides predictable recurring revenue
- Food tech market in PH growing 25% YoY (proven demand)

*Conditions for success:*
1. Secure minimum 20 restaurant partners by end of Year 1 (critical threshold)
2. Maintain 90%+ monthly retention rate after onboarding
3. Control support staff costs (avoid over-hiring beyond ₱6k/month)
4. Effective marketing & partnerships (achieve ₱3k/month target)
5. Scale to 40+ restaurants by Year 3 for sustainable profitability

Risk level: **Medium** (depends heavily on restaurant partner acquisition)

## Case Study

### Hypothetical Year 1 Scenario: 20 Restaurant Partners

**Participants:** Mix of small carinderias and mid-size restaurants in CDO

**Implementation Timeline:**
- Month 1-2: Beta testing with 3-5 restaurants
- Month 3-8: Onboarding of 15 additional restaurants  
- Month 9-12: Optimization and support

**Expected Outcomes:**

*For restaurants:*
- Order processing time reduced from 15 minutes to 8 minutes (47% faster)
- Manual order errors reduced from 8% to 1.5% per day
- Customer satisfaction score improves from 3.5/5 to 4.3/5
- Average restaurant reports 25% increase in repeat customers
- Monthly revenue increases ₱150,000-200,000 per restaurant

*For HapagTech:*
- Break-even achieved Month 11-12
- Subscriber retention: 90% (18 of 20 restaurants retained)
- Foundation built for Year 2 expansion to 28 restaurants
- Validated business model ready for external funding/investment

**Financial Impact:**
- Total Year 1 revenue: ₱600,000
- Total Year 1 cost: ₱594,400
- Net profit: ₱5,600
- Cumulative benefit to restaurant partners: ₱3,000,000 (20 restaurants × ₱150k increased revenue)

**Key Success Metric:** Customer ordering via app reaches 60% of total orders by Year 1 end, demonstrating strong product adoption and market fit.

**Short Case Study Summary**

If this system is used by **20 restaurant partners and their customers in Cagayan de Oro**, the impact is **significant operational efficiency and business growth across participating establishments**. Restaurants experience faster order processing, reduced manual errors, and improved customer satisfaction, leading to increased repeat customers and higher monthly revenue. At the platform level, the system demonstrates strong adoption, high retention rates, and near break-even financial performance within the first year, indicating a sustainable and scalable business model for future expansion.
